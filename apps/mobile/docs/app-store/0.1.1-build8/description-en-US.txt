Why do you love the songs you love?

Start with three songs that matter to you. Make a few tough choices. Meet a music critic with a point of view about what you keep choosing.

PICK BY INSTINCT
Choose the song you would keep. Skip a matchup when you don’t know the songs. The Critic follows your choices, notices patterns and makes room for exceptions.

GET A READ WORTH ARGUING WITH
See an initial reading after four answered choices, or keep going for the full interview. Explore the songs behind the interpretation, react when a take lands or misses, and tell the Critic what it overlooked.

SAME INSTINCTS. DIFFERENT DECADE.
After your original reading, explore an available decade. New song choices reveal what held up, what changed and where there still isn’t enough evidence. Your original reading stays as it was.

KEEP THE CONVERSATION GOING
Sign in to reopen your readings, resume an unfinished interview and return to your decade explorations. Share a reading only when you choose to publish it.

Your music is the starting point. The conversation is yours.
