# MusicDNA App Store screenshots

Status: capture brief, not final submission assets. Use the approved native
build after Alan reviews 0.1.1(7) in TestFlight. Do not submit web captures,
offline widget fixtures or invented model output as native app screenshots.

## Story and captions

| Order | Caption | Actual screen to capture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Your music has a point of view. | The opening-song interview, with a clean current response. |
| 2 | Pick the song you'd keep. | Two recognizable song choices, approved artwork/fallbacks and the Critic above them. |
| 3 | Meet your music critic. | A fresh, complete saved reading with its real twist and challenge. |
| 4 | A take worth arguing with. | The saved-reading conversation, with an actual grounded reply. |
| 5 | See what shaped the read. | Expanded receipts showing the actual supporting choices and exception. |

Use the real UI state and readable type. Keep the Critic and music prominent;
avoid filling screenshots with feedback buttons. Don't expose email addresses,
private IDs, debug controls, keys, development labels or another person's data.
Any account/name used for public screenshots needs approval. Keep only artwork
already approved for display; don't add unrelated or unverified album imagery.
Captions may describe the visible feature; they must not promise proven personality
accuracy, an unlimited catalog, playback, diagnosis or automatic improvement.

## Export sizes

Apple's current [screenshot specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications/)
require an iPhone Dynamic Island medium-display set: portrait 1179x2556 or
1206x2622. Choose one consistent size for the five-image sequence. Optional large
display sets accept 1260x2736, 1290x2796 or 1320x2868. If the submitted binary
supports iPad, also capture its required 13-inch set at 2064x2752 or 2048x2732.
Check the actual App Store Connect slots again at submission; device naming and
requirements can change. Never stretch a smaller screenshot to simulate a device.

## Capture checklist

1. Verify build/version, production origin and fresh successful reading on-device.
2. Use a clean approved account/run; no fabricated feedback or replacement prose.
3. Capture each native screen at the chosen device's actual resolution. Dismiss
   the keyboard unless it is useful to the screenshot's story.
4. Review all text/artwork/controls for clipping and legibility at App Store scale.
5. Export PNG or JPEG at the accepted dimensions, with meaningful ordered names.
6. Save untouched originals alongside any captioned exports. Record build number,
   capture device/resolution and date in the asset manifest.
7. Alan approves final images, copy and privacy/support links before submission.
